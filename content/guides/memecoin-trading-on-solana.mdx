## The Solana Memecoin Ecosystem

Understanding the infrastructure before you trade it:

### Pump.fun

The dominant launchpad for Solana memecoins. Anyone can create a token for ~0.02 SOL. The token starts on a bonding curve — as more people buy, the price rises automatically. When the token reaches ~$69,000 market cap (the bonding curve completion), it migrates to Raydium and gets real DEX liquidity.

The bonding curve mechanic matters because:
- Early buyers on the curve get the best prices
- Migration to Raydium often triggers a second pump (new buyers discover it on DEX)
- Most tokens fail to complete the bonding curve and die on Pump.fun

### Raydium

Solana's largest DEX by volume. All major Solana tokens trade here. Tokens that graduate from Pump.fun arrive here. This is where serious volume happens.

## Phase 1: Finding Opportunities

### Pump.fun Discovery

Go to pump.fun and filter by "Trending" or watch the live feed. Look for:

**Signs of a real opportunity**:
- Volume spike in the first 5–15 minutes
- Real community links (Twitter with actual engagement, not bought followers)
- Developer wallet hasn't sold (check immediately on Solscan)
- Unique concept — not a straight copy of an existing token
- Active Telegram with real people talking

**Red flags** (exit immediately):
- Copy-paste name of a trending token with minor modification
- Developer wallet sold within 5 minutes
- Top 10 wallets hold more than 30% combined
- No social presence at all

### GMGN Trending Feed

## Phase 2: Evaluating the Token

You have 60 seconds. Here's the checklist:

### On-Chain Checks (Solscan or GMGN)

**Developer wallet**:
- When did they buy?
- Have they sold anything?
- Do they hold a reasonable amount (5–10% = okay, 30%+ = red flag)?
- Is it a fresh wallet or one with history? Fresh wallets for every project = serial launcher, probably rug.

**Holder distribution**:
- Check top 10 holders. Sum their percentages. Over 30% combined = concentrated risk.
- Watch for "hidden" bundles — wallets that bought at exactly the same time through the same MEV bot. This is coordinated front-loading.

**Mint authority**:
- Is it renounced? If mint authority is not renounced, the creator can mint unlimited new tokens and dilute you.

**Freeze authority**:
- Should be disabled. Freeze authority lets the creator lock your tokens so you can't sell.

### Social Checks

**Twitter**:
- Does the account look real? Check follower growth graph — sudden spikes are bought.
- Is engagement organic? Real replies, not just generic "moon" comments.
- Is the concept original enough to spread virally?

**Telegram**:
- Is the community active before the call, or was it created minutes ago?
- Are admins responsive and transparent?
- Is there actual discussion happening or just price prediction spam?

## Phase 3: Execution

### Tool Selection

**Speed hierarchy** (fastest to slowest):
1. Telegram bot (Trojan, Soul Sniper) — sub-second execution
2. BullX terminal — 1–3 seconds
3. Pump.fun native — 3–10 seconds (acceptable for non-launch buys)
4. Raydium/Jupiter manual — slowest, worst for new launches

For launch sniping, bots are non-negotiable.

### Slippage Settings

New launches on Pump.fun are extremely volatile. Set slippage to:
- **Pump.fun launch snipe**: 15–25%
- **Raydium new listing**: 5–10%
- **Established token**: 1–3%

High slippage prevents your transaction from failing due to price movement during execution — but it also means you might pay slightly more than expected. Accept this tradeoff on new launches.

### Position Sizing — The Non-Negotiable Rule

**Maximum 5–10% of your trading capital per position.**

If you're trading with 5 SOL total:
- Small snipe: 0.1–0.25 SOL
- Medium conviction entry: 0.25–0.5 SOL
- High conviction (rare): 0.5–1 SOL

This lets you participate in 5–20 launches with one funding round. You need that many to find the one that goes 20x and covers all the zeros.

Never put more than 20% in any single memecoin. The math doesn't work if you're concentrated and wrong.

## Phase 4: Managing the Position

### The First 30 Minutes Are Critical

Watch the following after entering:

**Volume trend**: Is it accelerating or decelerating? Accelerating volume with price rising = healthy. Volume dying with price stalling = prepare to exit.

**Developer wallet**: Check Solscan. The moment the dev sells, you consider exiting. First significant dev sell is often the signal that the launch is over.

**Holder count**: Rising holder count = organic adoption. Falling holder count after initial spike = paper hands leaving, usually bad.

**Social momentum**: Is Twitter talking about it? Is the Telegram growing or dying?

### Know Your Scenarios Before You Enter

Before you buy, decide:
- **If it goes to 2x**: I sell 30% (recover most of my investment)
- **If it goes to 5x**: I sell another 30% (playing with house money)
- **If it drops 50% immediately**: I exit (rug or dead launch)
- **If it stays flat for 2 hours**: I exit (dead momentum, opportunity cost)

Having these rules preset removes emotion from decisions.

## Phase 5: Taking Profits

This is where most people fail. Greed kills memecoin P&L.

### The Layered Exit Strategy

**2–3x**: Sell 25–30%. You've recovered most of your investment. Now you're playing with profit.

**5–10x**: Sell another 25–30%. At this point you're fully in profit regardless of what happens to the remaining position.

**10x+**: Let the remaining 40–50% run. Set a trailing stop. If you sell everything at 10x and it goes to 100x, you didn't lose money — you just made less than maximum. That's acceptable.

**Never hold to zero for a degenerate position.** The mental accounting of "it was only 0.2 SOL" adds up across 50 trades.

### Trailing Stops

Most Telegram bots support trailing stops. Set a 20–25% trailing stop once a position is in significant profit:
- If token hits 5x and then drops 25%, bot sells automatically
- If token continues to 10x, the stop adjusts up with it

This captures most of the move without requiring you to watch every minute.

## Red Flags That Mean Exit Immediately

No matter what profit is showing, exit when you see:

1. **Developer sells a significant chunk** (>20% of their holdings)
2. **Telegram admin goes quiet** or starts deleting critical messages
3. **Volume collapses suddenly** on a previously active token
4. **Smart wallets start exiting** (visible on GMGN wallet tracking)
5. **Token contract gets paused** (rare, but instant rug indicator)
6. **Twitter account gets deleted or renamed**

1. **Find**: Pump.fun trending, GMGN feed, Twitter alpha, wallet copy
2. **Evaluate**: Dev wallet, holder distribution, mint authority, social presence (60 seconds)
