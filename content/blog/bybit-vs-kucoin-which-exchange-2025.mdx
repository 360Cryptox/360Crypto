Here's the full breakdown.

## The One-Line Summary

**Bybit**: Best for derivatives traders, copy traders, and anyone who wants the deepest liquidity and biggest deposit bonuses.

**KuCoin**: Best for altcoin hunters, early-stage gem seekers, and traders who want access to 700+ coins including projects not yet listed on major exchanges.

## Fees

### Bybit
- **Spot**: 0.1% maker / 0.1% taker (standard), reduces to 0.08%/0.10% with high volume
- **Derivatives**: 0.01% maker / 0.06% taker — extremely competitive
- **Withdrawal fees**: Vary by coin; generally mid-range

### KuCoin
- **Spot**: 0.1% maker / 0.1% taker (standard), reduces to 0% maker with KCS holdings
- **Derivatives**: 0.02% maker / 0.06% taker
- **KCS discount**: Hold KCS token to get 20% fee discount on all trades

**Winner**: Tie for standard rates. KuCoin has the edge if you hold KCS. Bybit wins on derivatives fees at high volume.

## Coin Selection

This is where the gap is obvious.

**Bybit**: ~900 spot trading pairs. All major coins covered. Solid coverage of mid-cap alts. Strong on established projects.

**KuCoin**: 700+ coins, 1,200+ trading pairs. KuCoin lists projects much earlier in their lifecycle — often within days of launch. It's been called "the people's exchange" specifically because it gives retail access to gems before they hit Binance or Bybit.

If you found a project early in 2024 and it wasn't on Bybit, there was a good chance it was already on KuCoin.

**Winner**: KuCoin — not even close. The early listing advantage is the exchange's defining feature.

## Derivatives & Futures

Bybit built its reputation on derivatives. It offers:
- **Perpetual contracts** with up to 100x leverage

KuCoin offers perpetuals and futures, but the depth and variety don't match Bybit. Order book is thinner on less popular pairs.

**Winner**: Bybit — it's a derivatives-first exchange and it shows.
