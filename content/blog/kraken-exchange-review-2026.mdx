## Fees

Kraken uses a maker/taker fee model that drops with volume.

**Spot trading (Kraken Pro):**
| 30-Day Volume | Maker | Taker |
|---|---|---|
| < $50K | 0.25% | 0.40% |
| $50K–$100K | 0.20% | 0.35% |
| $100K–$250K | 0.14% | 0.24% |
| $250K–$500K | 0.12% | 0.22% |
| $1M+ | 0.02% | 0.10% |

At standard volume, Kraken Pro is significantly cheaper than Coinbase Pro and competitive with Bybit.

**Futures:**
- Maker: 0.02%
- Taker: 0.05%

These are among the lowest futures fees available to US traders.

**Withdrawal fees**: Vary by asset. Crypto withdrawals are flat fee per transaction. Fiat withdrawals via ACH (US) are free; wire transfers cost $5.

## Kraken Basic vs Kraken Pro

**Kraken Basic** is the standard UI — simple buy/sell with market orders. Good for beginners. Fees are slightly higher.

**Kraken Pro** is the full trading terminal with:
- Limit, market, stop-loss, take-profit orders
- Advanced charting via TradingView integration
- API access for bots
- Significantly lower fees (as shown above)

Switching between them is instant. Start with Basic, upgrade to Pro when you're ready.
