## Why Telegram Bots Are Essential for Solana Trading

Solana's speed (400ms block times) is a feature — but only if you can keep up. The bottleneck is you, not the chain.

Telegram bots remove the human UI layer. Instead of: open browser → connect wallet → approve → swap → confirm → wait, you do: `/buy [contract] 0.5 SOL` → done in under a second.

For memecoin launches specifically, bot users consistently get better average entry prices than manual traders. The difference compounds fast.

## How to Set Up a Telegram Bot (Trojan Example)

1. Click the Trojan Bot link and start the bot in Telegram
2. Generate a Solana wallet inside the bot (or import your existing wallet's private key)
3. Transfer SOL to the bot wallet address shown
4. Set your default buy amount and slippage in Settings
5. To buy a token: send the contract address to the bot and tap Buy

**Security note**: Never share your private key with anyone. Only import a dedicated trading wallet — not your main cold storage wallet. Keep your hardware wallet completely separate from any bot.
